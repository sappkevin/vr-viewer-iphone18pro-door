# iPhone 18 Pro camera fit test

This US Letter template checks the size and position of the VR viewer door's camera window against the phone's camera plateau. Print the PDF at **100% / Actual Size**, with page scaling disabled. Measure both rulers: the horizontal ruler must be exactly 100 mm and the vertical ruler exactly 50 mm. If either is wrong, correct the print scale and print again.

Align the phone outline with the back of the phone. The plateau should sit inside the bold window with approximately 1.5 mm clearance all around. Optionally, cut out the bold window to check the fit.

This test covers XY fit only. The raised housing and its depth are 3D features and cannot be tested on paper.
